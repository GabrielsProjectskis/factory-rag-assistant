# Cooling water pumps 20-PMP-001 and 20-PMP-002 - Operating and maintenance manual

DEMO DOCUMENT - fictional content for demonstration only. Not for use on real equipment.

## Purpose

Pumps 20-PMP-001 (duty) and 20-PMP-002 (standby) circulate water through the closed cooling loop 20-CWS-001. The loop removes heat from the hydraulic press 40-HPR-001 and the air compressor 30-CMP-001 and rejects it in plate heat exchanger 20-HEX-001. Only one pump runs at a time. If loop pressure drops below 2.0 bar for more than 5 seconds, the standby pump starts automatically.

## Normal operating values

Discharge pressure: 3.2 to 3.8 bar. Flow: 40 to 46 m3/h. Motor current: 13 to 15 A. Bearing temperature: below 70 degrees C. Supply water temperature to consumers: 22 to 28 degrees C.

## Starting a pump

1. Check that the suction valve is fully open and the discharge valve is about 20 percent open.
2. Check the loop expansion vessel pressure is between 1.2 and 1.5 bar.
3. Vent the pump casing using the vent plug until water flows without air.
4. Select the pump on the local panel and press START.
5. Open the discharge valve slowly over 30 seconds until discharge pressure is in the normal range.
6. Never run a pump against a closed discharge valve for more than 1 minute.

## Weekly checks

Look for leaks at the mechanical seal (a few drops per hour is acceptable, a steady drip is not). Listen for unusual noise or vibration. Record discharge pressure and motor current in the log sheet. Swap duty and standby pump every Monday so both pumps collect equal running hours.

## Maintenance schedule

Every 3 months: grease motor bearings with 8 g of lithium grease per bearing. Check coupling alignment and guard.
Every 12 months: replace the mechanical seal, inspect the impeller for wear and cavitation damage, test the automatic changeover to the standby pump.
Every 4 years: full overhaul with bearing replacement.

## Safety before maintenance

Before any work on a pump: stop the pump, switch off and lock out the isolator on the local panel with your personal lock and tag, close the suction and discharge valves, and drain the casing through the drain plug. Water can be up to 45 degrees C. Wear safety glasses and gloves.

## Troubleshooting

Low discharge pressure: air in the casing (vent the pump), clogged suction strainer (clean it), or worn impeller.
Pump does not start: isolator locked out, motor protection tripped (reset once only, then call an electrician), or low-level interlock on the expansion vessel.
High bearing temperature: too much or too little grease, or misalignment. Stop the pump and change over to the standby pump.
Noise like gravel in the pump: cavitation. Check that the suction valve is fully open and the strainer is clean.
