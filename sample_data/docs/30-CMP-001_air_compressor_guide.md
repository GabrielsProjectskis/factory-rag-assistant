# Rotary screw air compressor 30-CMP-001 - Quick guide

DEMO DOCUMENT - fictional content for demonstration only. Not for use on real equipment.

## What it is for

Compressor 30-CMP-001 supplies instrument and plant air at 7.0 bar to the whole factory, including the clamp cylinders of hydraulic press 40-HPR-001 and the grippers of palletiser 50-PAL-001. It is water cooled from the cooling water system 20-CWS-001. If plant air pressure falls below 5.5 bar the press and the palletiser stop automatically.

## Daily checks

Check the oil level in the sight glass with the compressor stopped and depressurised: the level must be between the two marks. Check the display for warnings. Drain condensate from the air receiver if the automatic drain is not working. Normal outlet temperature is 75 to 95 degrees C; the compressor trips at 110 degrees C.

## Maintenance intervals

Every 2000 running hours: replace the air intake filter and the oil filter.
Every 4000 running hours: change the compressor oil (18 litres of synthetic screw compressor oil) and replace the oil separator element.
Every year: have the safety valve tested by a certified inspector and check the drive belt tension.

## Resetting a high temperature trip

1. Do not restart immediately. Find the cause first.
2. Check that cooling water is flowing: one of the pumps 20-PMP-001 or 20-PMP-002 must be running and the cooling water inlet valve on the compressor must be open.
3. Check the oil level and top up if it is low.
4. Check that the oil cooler is not blocked.
5. Wait until the outlet temperature on the display is below 60 degrees C, then press RESET and START.
6. If the compressor trips again within one hour, leave it stopped and call maintenance.

## Safety

The compressor starts and stops automatically. Before opening any panel: press STOP, wait for the internal pressure to reach 0 bar on the display, switch off and lock out the main isolator, and close the outlet valve. Hot surfaces and hot oil can cause burns. Hearing protection is required inside the compressor room.
