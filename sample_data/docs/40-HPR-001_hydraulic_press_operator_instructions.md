# Hydraulic press 40-HPR-001 - Operator instructions

DEMO DOCUMENT - fictional content for demonstration only. Not for use on real equipment.

## Purpose

Press 40-HPR-001 is the 250 tonne hydraulic press that forms the product on line 1. Material arrives from infeed conveyor 10-CNV-001. Finished product leaves towards robot palletiser 50-PAL-001. The hydraulic oil is cooled by the cooling water system 20-CWS-001 and the clamps use plant air from compressor 30-CMP-001.

## Start-up at the beginning of a shift

1. Walk around the press and check that the guards and light curtains are in place and undamaged.
2. Check the hydraulic oil level (between MIN and MAX on the tank gauge) and look for leaks under the power pack.
3. Check that plant air pressure is at least 6.0 bar and cooling water is flowing.
4. Switch on the main isolator and reset the safety relay with the blue button.
5. Start the hydraulic pump and let the oil warm up to at least 30 degrees C before pressing.
6. Run three cycles without material in setup mode and test the light curtain by breaking the beam: the ram must stop immediately.

## Operating limits

Working pressure 180 to 210 bar. Hydraulic oil temperature 30 to 55 degrees C; the press stops at 65 degrees C. Do not exceed 12 cycles per minute.

## Common alarms

A101 Oil temperature high: check cooling water supply from 20-CWS-001 and the oil cooler.
A115 Light curtain interrupted: remove the obstruction and reset with the blue button.
A130 Air pressure low: check compressor 30-CMP-001.
A142 Oil filter clogged: call maintenance to replace the return filter element.

## Maintenance by the operator

Each shift: wipe the ram and guides clean and check for oil leaks. Each week: grease the four guide columns at the grease nipples (2 strokes each). Hydraulic oil and filters are changed by maintenance every 4000 hours or every 12 months.

## Safety

Never reach into the die area while the pump is running. For die changes or cleaning inside the press: lower the ram onto the safety blocks, stop the pump, switch off and lock out the main isolator, and release stored pressure with the accumulator bleed valve until the gauge reads 0 bar.
